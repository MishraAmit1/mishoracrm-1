0. YEH KYON — CONTEXT
============================================================
Loyalty module (points, tiers, redemption, campaigns/coupons, birthday/
referral/win-back, WhatsApp self-check + QR welcome capture, public OTP
"check my rewards" page, category segmentation, flash-days, reward catalog,
top-customers) 2026-08-31 ko COMPLETE ho gaya. Sab per-tenant.

Reference dekha: stampzo.in — digital stamp-card platform for local shops
(salon/cafe/bakery/gym). Uska model:
  - QR scan → digital card phone ke home screen pe (PWA, koi app nahi)
  - Stamp per visit; card full → free reward ("buy 4 get 5th free")
  - Customer ek baar login karke apna card kahin bhi dekh sakta hai (PIN recovery)
  - Merchant dashboard: members, visits, unlocked rewards, live scan feed,
    30-day trends
  - Staff "scan → stamp" 2-second flow
  - QR Kit: window poster, counter stand, table tent, board, flyer

DECISION (owner ne liya):
  Customer ko ek GLOBAL login chahiye. Ravi (ek phone) agar 3 shops
  (salon, cafe, kirana) ka customer hai, toh wo EK BAAR login kare aur
  teeno shops ke points + offers + stamps ek "wallet" mein dekhe. Customer
  ko multiple-shop choice mile.

  IMPORTANT — data isolation nahi todni: har shop apna data (points, tier,
  stamps, offers, visit history) alag rakhta hai. Salon Ravi ke cafe ke
  points NAHI dekh sakta. Sirf CUSTOMER ko apna sab kuch ek jagah dikhta
  hai. Yeh multi-tenant CRM ka non-negotiable rule hai.

  Agar 3 shops ek hi business ke branches hain (ek owner) → wo alag case
  hai (ek tenant, multiple locations, ek Contact, shared points, visit pe
  location tag). Yeh doc us case ke baare mein NAHI hai.

============================================================
Yeh feature EK NAYA MODULE hai: `customer_portal`. Bilkul `loyalty` module
jaisa gate hoga (dekho customer-loyalty.txt Section 1). Har tenant ke liye
by-default OFF. Tabhi ON jab:
  a) Tenant ke PLAN mein yeh feature ho — Plan.features['customer_portal'] === true

  b) Superadmin ne us tenant ke liye manually force-ON kiya ho —
     settings['modules']['customer_portal'] === true (tri-state override).
Warna OFF. Superadmin force-OFF bhi kar sakta hai (=== false).

  → Tenant::hasModuleEnabled('customer_portal') — exact same tri-state logic
    jo hasModuleEnabled('loyalty') use karta hai. Koi naya method nahi,
    same helper, naya module key.

DEPENDENCY: `customer_portal` bina `loyalty` ke bekaar hai (wallet loyalty
data hi dikhata hai). Rule: wallet card kisi tenant ka tabhi dikhega jab
us tenant ke liye DONO ON hon — hasModuleEnabled('loyalty') AND

0B. DESIGN REVIEW — 5 GAPS AUR UNKE RESOLUTIONS (2026-09-01)
============================================================
Pehla draft review karne pe 5 holes mile. Har ek ka fix decide ho gaya —
neeche summary, detail relevant section mein.

GAP 1 — Counter pe redeem/stamp karna abhi bhi jhanjhat. Wallet sirf
  read-only info dikhata hai; "show code at counter" = staff ko code type
  karna. Stampzo ka "customer QR dikhaye, staff scan kare, 2 sec" wala flow
  design nahi tha.
  RESOLUTION → Section 4A (naya):
    * Wallet QR = Laravel temporary SIGNED URL (payload: customer_id,
      90-sec expiry). Phone number QR mein NAHI. Fake-proof.
    * Alag "counter app" nahi — existing invoice ke Loyalty panel mein
      [Scan Customer] button (camera + jsQR) → contact auto-fill + one-tap
      apply (points / offer / reward).
    * Bill-nahi-banane wale shops (kirana) ke liye standalone
      /loyalty/counter page: scan/type → card → [+1 Stamp] / [Redeem] one-tap.
    * Offer bhi wallet mein QR → staff coupon field pe scan.
    * permission: naya `loyalty.stamp` (cashier ko bina full manage diye).

GAP 2 — Phone = sole identity. Shop staff ne galat number type kiya
  (Ravi ka number Sunita ke Contact pe) → Sunita login karke Ravi ka data
  dekh legi. Plus email-only Contacts (jinke paas phone nahi) kabhi link
  nahi honge.
  RESOLUTION → Section 2 + Section 12:
    * OTP hi ownership proof hai (WhatsApp/banking model) — fully
      preventable nahi, par:
    * `contacts.phone_verified` bool. Staff-created walk-in = false;
      customer OTP-link = true.
    * HISTORY wale Contact (redemptions, ya lifetime_points > 100, ya
      linked paid invoice) → auto-link NAHI. Wallet mein "We found an
      account at {shop} — {points} points. Is this you? [Yes] [Not me]".
      "Not me" → unlink + tenant ko "possible wrong number on contact #X"
      alert. Thin Contact (0-50 welcome pts, no history) → silently link.
    * Tenant "Needs review" list — flagged/unverified numbers, merge/fix
      (MergeService already hai).
    * EMAIL = doosri linking key. CustomerLinkService email pe bhi match
      (case-insensitive). Login pe optional "add your email to find more
      cards" → email OTP → email-only Contacts link.

GAP 3 — Phone matching `RIGHT(REPLACE(REPLACE(...)))` har login pe poore
  `contacts` table ka full scan (expression pe index nahi). 10 lakh
  contacts pe ~1-2 sec/login → peak pe CRM hang.
  RESOLUTION → Section 1 + Section 2 + Section 9:
    * NEW `contacts.phone_normalized` (varchar(15), index
      (tenant_id, phone_normalized) + plain (phone_normalized)).
      Contact `saving` observer → substr(preg_replace('/\D/','',phone), -10).
      Backfill migration (PHP chunks).
    * SAARE phone lookups → `WHERE phone_normalized = ?` (index hit).
      Refactor EXISTING bhi: WhatsappChatbotService::findContactByWaId,
      loyalty self-check, welcome, Public\RewardsController::matchContact,
      Api\Tenant\LoyaltyController::lookup.
    * `customers.phone` bhi normalized store + unique index.

GAP 4 — Section 3 ka login flow pehle Customer::firstOrCreate karta tha,
  phir OTP. Random numbers daalne se lakhon junk Customer rows + OTP spam +
  enumeration.
  RESOLUTION → Section 3:
    * request-otp REORDER: (1) normalize (2) koi Contact match karta hai
      kisi bhi tenant mein? NO → generic "if you're a member of a
      participating shop, a code was sent" (kuch nahi bheja, small fake
      delay), Customer create NAHI. YES → firstOrCreate + OTP.
    * Response hamesha same (existence leak nahi).
    * Rate limit: throttle:5,10 per IP + per-phone max 3 OTP/hour + 60s resend.
    * Weekly cleanup command: Customer with last_login_at NULL AND
      created_at < 30d AND zero linked contacts → soft-delete.
    * Superadmin Customers list default filter: ">=1 linked shop" ya
      "logged in at least once".

GAP 5 — Wallet card design points-first tha. Pure-stamps shop (kirana:
  "10 visits = 1 free") ka card "0 points, no tier" = toota hua dikhta.
  RESOLUTION → Section 4 + Section 5:
    * LoyaltyService::snapshot() extend → stamp_count, stamps_required,
      stamp_reward, mode, rewards_unlocked bhi.
    * Card component `mode` pe primary display switch:
        points → bada number = points + tier badge + "₹X redeemable"
        stamps → bada visual = stamp grid (● ● ● ○ ○ ...) + "N more for {reward}"
        both   → stamp grid upar, points+tier compact neeche
    * Empty state per mode ("Start earning on your next visit" /
      "0/10 — visit to start your card").

1. CORE ARCHITECTURE — GLOBAL CUSTOMER IDENTITY
============================================================
Naya platform-level actor: "Customer" — `User` (staff) jaisa hi, par
customers ke liye, kisi tenant ka nahi.

NEW TABLE — `customers` (tenant scope se BAHAR, global):
  - id
  - phone            — identity key. Store NORMALIZED (last-10 digits,
                       preg_replace('/\D/','',...)). UNIQUE index.
  - name
  - email            — nullable. Case-insensitive, second linking key
                       (Gap 2). email_verified_at nullable.
  - pin_hash         — nullable (bcrypt). Optional fast re-login.
  - last_login_at    — nullable
  - blocked_at       — nullable (superadmin abuse control)
  - timestamps

NEW COLUMN — `contacts.customer_id` → customers.id (nullable FK, nullOnDelete):
  Ek Customer ↔ KAI Contact rows (har tenant ka apna ek Contact). Yeh link
  hi wallet ko power deta hai.

NEW COLUMN — `contacts.phone_normalized` varchar(15) nullable (Gap 3):
  substr(preg_replace('/\D/','', phone), -10). Contact `saving` observer
  se auto-populate. Index: (tenant_id, phone_normalized) AND (phone_normalized).
  Backfill migration (PHP chunks, portability). SAARE phone lookups isi
  column pe (naye + existing WhatsApp/loyalty code refactor).

NEW COLUMN — `contacts.phone_verified` boolean default false (Gap 2):
  Staff-created walk-in = false. Customer OTP-link confirm = true. Tenant
  bhi manually confirm kar sakta hai. Wallet auto-link logic isko dekhta hai.

MODEL: App\Models\Customer
  - NO BelongsToTenant trait (global).
  - implements Authenticatable (Section 3).
  - relations: contacts() hasMany(Contact::class)
  - helper: walletContacts() — Contacts jinke tenant ke liye DONO modules ON
    (hasModuleEnabled('loyalty') AND hasModuleEnabled('customer_portal'))
    AND (loyalty_lifetime_points > 0 OR stamp_count > 0 OR koi campaign
    recipient) — yani "card dikhane layak" (Section 0A.1 + Gap 5).
  - helper: pendingContacts() — history-wale unverified linked Contacts jo
    "Is this you?" confirmation ka wait kar rahe hain (Gap 2).

Contact model:
  - add customer() belongsTo(Customer::class)
  - customer_id / phone_normalized / phone_verified — NONE fillable
    (observer / link service owns them).
  - `saving` observer: phone_normalized recompute jab phone dirty ho.

2. LINKING — CUSTOMER ↔ CONTACTS
============================================================
Ek service: App\Services\CustomerLinkService

  a) linkByPhone(Customer $customer): array
     Sab tenants ke Contacts (withoutGlobalScopes) jinka
     phone_normalized = customer.phone (INDEX HIT, Gap 3) AND customer_id
     NULL ho. Email set ho toh contacts.email (lower) = customer.email
     wale bhi. Har match:
       - thin Contact (loyalty_lifetime_points <= 100 AND koi redemption/
         paid-invoice nahi AND stamp history nahi) → seedha link + treat
         as verified-by-otp.
       - history-wala Contact → link kar do PAR "pending confirmation"
         mark (customer ko wallet mein "Is this you?" — Gap 2). Confirm
         hone tak card SHOW nahi hota.
     Return: ['linked' => n, 'pending' => m]. Login/create ke waqt call.

  b) attachContactToCustomer(Contact $contact): void
     Ek naye/updated Contact ke liye — agar us phone_normalized (ya email)
     ka Customer already exist karta hai toh link kar do (naya Customer
     NAHI banao yahan — Customer sirf customer khud login karke banata hai).
     Same thin/history rule jaisa (a). Call sites:
       - ContactController::store / update (phone/email set/change hone pe)
       - Public\TicketController, Public\AppointmentController jab naya
         Contact banate hain
       - WhatsappChatbotService::enrolFromWhatsapp (QR/WhatsApp join)

  c) confirmContact(Customer, Contact, bool $isMe): void
     Wallet ke "Is this you? [Yes]/[Not me]" se.
       - Yes → phone_verified = true (ya email), pending hataao, card show.
       - Not me → customer_id NULL, tenant ko notification
         "loyalty.wrong_number" (contact #X — customer denied ownership),
         tenant "Needs review" list mein.

  d) backfill: skip for launch (Customers abhi bane hi nahi).

Guard: phone_normalized null/short (<10) → koi phone link nahi (email try).
Do Contacts same tenant same phone → dono evaluate (history rule per-contact).

============================================================

Customer model implements Illuminate\Contracts\Auth\Authenticatable

LOGIN FLOW (passwordless):
  Step 1 — request OTP (ORDER IMPORTANT — Gap 4):
    1. Phone normalize (last-10).
    2. Rate check: throttle:5,10 per IP + per-phone max 3 OTP/hour (60s resend).
    3. CONTACT-MATCH CHECK — kisi bhi tenant ka Contact hai jiska
       phone_normalized = X? (withoutGlobalScopes, INDEX HIT.)
         NO  → generic reply "If you're a member of a participating shop,
               we've sent a code." Kuch NAHI bheja. Customer create NAHI.
               (chhota fake delay taaki timing se leak na ho.)
         YES → Customer::firstOrCreate(['phone' => X]) →
               CustomerLinkService::linkByPhone($customer) →
               OTP generate (6-digit, hashed, 10-min TTL, 5-attempt lock).
    4. OTP deliver: WhatsApp (agar match hue Contacts mein se kisi ka tenant
       WhatsApp-connected — pehla milta) ELSE Email (customer.email ya kisi
       linked Contact ka email). SMS nahi hai.
    5. Response HAMESHA same (existence leak nahi).
  Step 2 — code verify:
    - Match → session login (Auth::guard('customer')->login($customer, remember: true)).
    - last_login_at = now().
    - Agar pin_hash NULL → optionally "set a 4-6 digit PIN for faster login"
      screen (skippable).
    - Agar linkByPhone ne "pending" Contacts diye (Gap 2) → wallet pe seedha
      "Is this you?" cards dikhao.
  Fast re-login (agar PIN set):
    - phone + PIN → verify → login. PIN galat 5 baar → PIN lock, OTP par
      wapas. throttle:5,10 per IP dono endpoints pe.

JUNK CLEANUP (Gap 4):
  Weekly command `customers:prune` — Customer jinka last_login_at NULL AND
  created_at < 30 din AND zero linked contacts → soft-delete. (Edge: Contact
  delete hone se orphan ho gaya wala bhi.)

SESSION:
  - remember cookie long-lived (30 din+) — PWA use case.

  - Logout route.

  'stamp_per'        => 'visit'         // visit | invoice | amount
  'stamp_amount'     => 100             // agar stamp_per=amount: har ₹100 pe 1 stamp
  'stamp_expiry_days'=> 0               // partial card N din baad reset (0 = never)

CONTACT columns (naya migration):
  - stamp_count          unsignedInt default 0   (current card progress)
  - stamps_lifetime      unsignedInt default 0
  - stamp_rewards_earned unsignedInt default 0
  - stamp_updated_at     timestamp nullable

LEDGER: LoyaltyTransaction.type mein 'stamp' aur 'stamp_reward' add.
  (Ya alag table loyalty_stamp_events — par LoyaltyTransaction reuse simpler,
  points column ko stamp count ki tarah use karo type='stamp' pe.)

AWARD:
  - stamp_per=visit/invoice → InvoiceController::awardForInvoice ke saath-saath
    ek stamp bhi (agar mode stamps/both). Ya visit = "invoice paid" event.
  - stamp_per=amount → floor(paid / stamp_amount) stamps.
  - Manual "give stamp" → Section 4A ka /loyalty/counter page.
  - stamp_count >= stamps_required → stamp_reward "unlocked", stamp_count -=
    stamps_required, stamp_rewards_earned++, notify customer
    ("Your card is full! Free coffee unlocked. Show this at the counter.")
  - Anti-abuse: max 1 stamp per contact per day (ya tenant-set) — like
    max_points_per_day.

REDEEM (unlocked stamp reward):
  - Staff-side (Section 4A): invoice Loyalty panel ya /loyalty/counter pe
    "Redeem free reward" → stamp_rewards_earned--, note on invoice
    (loyalty_reward field reuse), free item staff add kare. Reverse on delete.

STAMP EXPIRY (agar stamp_expiry_days > 0):
  - Scheduled command `loyalty:expire-stamps` (daily) — koi partial card
    (stamp_count > 0) jiska stamp_updated_at > N din purana → stamp_count = 0,
    ek 'stamp' type ledger row (negative) note ke saath. Notify optional.
  - customer-loyalty.txt ke `loyalty:expire-points` jaisa hi pattern.

12. GUARDRAILS / RISKS (dhyan rakhna)
============================================================
  - TENANT ISOLATION: customers table + wallet + /counter/scan queries kabhi
    bhi tenant User ke through nahi. Har Contact fetch explicit tenant_id +
    withoutGlobalScopes. Ek tenant kabhi customers enumerate na kar paaye.
    Wallet {tenant} + counter scan: STAFF ke apne tenant ke liye hi Contact
    resolve, warna 404.
  - PHONE = IDENTITY (Gap 2): shop staff typo se Ravi ka number Sunita ke
    Contact pe → jo number control karta hai wo us Contact ka "owner" ban
    jaata hai (WhatsApp/banking jaisa model). Mitigations:
      * history-wale Contact pe "Is this you?" confirm (auto-link nahi)
      * "not me" → unlink + tenant alert + Needs-review list
      * phone_verified flag; email = second key
    Fully eliminate nahi hota — accept + document.
  - NUMBER CHANGE: launch pe unsupported (support ticket). Baad mein: OTP to
    old + new → Customer.phone update + re-link.
  - phone_normalized: har save pe recompute (observer). Backfill ke baad
    verify koi Contact chhoot na jaye. Index zaroori (Gap 3 ka pura point).
  - SIGNED QR (4A): 90-sec expiry + consume-once. APP_KEY leak = QR forge —
    standard Laravel risk, rotate on breach.
  - PRIVACY: customer ab apna cross-shop data dekh sakta hai — data-subject
    request + "delete my account" surface (§10 #11).
  - OTP DELIVERY: SMS nahi hai. Jinke paas WhatsApp-connected shop nahi aur
    email nahi → login nahi kar payenge. Portal tab hi useful jab kisi shop
    ne unhe Contact banaya ho.
  - blocked_at customer → login block, wallet 403.
  - Rate limiting har auth + OTP + counter-scan endpoint pe.
  - JUNK: request-otp pe Customer create SIRF Contact-match ke baad (Gap 4)
    + customers:prune.
