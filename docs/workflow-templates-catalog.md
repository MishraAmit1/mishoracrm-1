## Template 1 — Lead Auto Follow-Up

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Lead Auto Follow-Up |
| **Category** | lead |
| **Icon** | zap |
| **Color** | blue |
| **Sort Order** | 1 |
| **Suitable For** | Real Estate, EdTech, Finance, All Businesses |

**Description:**
```
Naya lead aate hi automatically WhatsApp + email sequence shuru ho jaata hai — bina kisi manual kaam ke.
```

```
Unresponsive leads ko flag karo
```
