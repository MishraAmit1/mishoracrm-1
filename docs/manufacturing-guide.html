Aapki Factory ka System — Kya, Kyun, Kaise

Ye guide bina technical jargon ke samjhaati hai ki system ka har hissa kya karta hai, aapke business ko kyun chahiye, aur roz ke kaam mein kahan lagta hai. Saath mein aage kya add hona baaki hai.

Taiyaar: 29 Aug 2026 Chhoti se mid manufacturing company ke liye

1Poori Kahani Ek Nazar Mein

System aapki factory ke "material ki poori zindagi" ko sambhaalta hai — material maangne se le kar, kharidne, quality check ke saath receiving, sahi lagat ke saath stock, phir production, finished maal, aur bechne tak. Saath mein vendor ko paisa aur GST.

2Kharid ka Hissa

Material Maangna → Order → Receiving

Material ki Maang

Kya hai
Factory ke andar koi bhi — store keeper, production incharge — "mujhe itna material chahiye" ki parchi daalta hai. Manager haan/naa karta hai. Haan hone par order ki draft copy apne aap ban jaati hai.
Kyun chahiye
Kuch bhi kharidne se pehle ek manzoori ka step. Bina iske har koi apne mann se vendor ko order de de — paisa aur control dono haath se nikal jaate hain.
Kahan / Kab
Sabse pehla step. Jab kisi ko lage stock kam pad raha hai.

Example

Welding shop wale ne dekha rod khatam ho raha hai → 50 kg ki maang daali → manager ne approve kiya → order ki draft copy taiyaar.

Kharid ka Order

Kya hai
Vendor ko bheja jaane wala pakka order. Ek hi cheez ke liye 2–3 vendors ke rate mangwa ke compare kar sakte ho, sabse achha chun lo — uske rate order pe apne aap lag jaate hain. Order ka PDF ban ke vendor ko email chala jaata hai.
Kyun chahiye
Vendor kaunsa aur kis rate pe — sab likhit record. Baad mein "itna maal aana baaki hai" isi order ke against dikhta hai.
Kahan / Kab
Maang approve hone ke baad. Purchase department banata hai.

Example

Steel rod ke liye 3 vendors — A ₹95/kg, B ₹98, C ₹92. C chuna → uska rate order pe lag gaya, order C ko email ho gaya.

Maal ki Receiving + Quality Check

Kya hai
Jab vendor ka maal aata hai, har delivery ki ek receiving entry banti hai. Har item pe likhte ho: kitna aaya, kitna theek nikla, kitna kharab. Sirf theek wala maal stock mein jaata hai. Kharab wala reason ke saath record ho jaata hai.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle jitna aaya poora stock mein chadh jaata tha — kharab maal bhi. Ab quality check ka step hai: 100 aaye, 92 pass, 8 reject. Reject ka record vendor se baat karne ke liye rehta hai.
Kahan / Kab
Jab vendor ka truck aata hai. Store keeper entry karta hai, quality wala pass/fail decide karta hai.

Example

100 rod order the, 100 aaye, 12 tede-mede nikle → 88 stock mein chadhe, 12 "rejected — bent" note ho gaye. Vendor ko batao ya agle bill mein adjust karo.

3Stock aur Lagat

Sahi Lagat — Apne Aap Average Cost

Kya hai
Har item ki asli khareed lagat system khud nikaalta hai. Jab bhi naya stock alag rate pe aata hai, purani aur nayi lagat ka average ho ke item ki cost update ho jaati hai.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle system item ki bechne wali keemat ko hi lagat maan raha tha — galat. Isse production ki lagat aur stock ki value dono galat aati thi. Ab jo actual paisa diya, wahi lagat.
Kahan / Kab
Automatic — maal receive hone par, production poori hone par.

Example

10 kg rod @ ₹50 pehle se the (₹500). 10 kg @ ₹70 naye aaye (₹700). Ab 20 kg, item ki cost ₹60 — production isi ₹60 pe cost lagayega.

Batch aur Expiry Tracking

Kya hai
Jab bhi stock aata hai, ek "batch" (lot) banta hai — batch number, aane ki date, aur agar lagu ho to expiry date. Use karte time sabse pehle expire hone wala batch pehle nikalta hai.
Kyun chahiye
Traceability — kaunsa lot kis order mein gaya. Aur chemical / food / dawai jaise items ki expiry manage hoti hai. Expiry paas aane par manager ko alert.
Kahan / Kab
Automatic har baar stock aane / jaane par.

Kam Stock ki Chetavani

Kya hai
Har item pe ek minimum level set karo. Stock us level pe pahunchte hi manager ko notification. Ek click pe wahin se material ki maang bhi ban jaati hai — banne wale product ke liye uske saare raw material apne aap suggest ho jaate hain.
Kyun chahiye
Stock khatam hone se production ruk jaati hai. Ye pehle hi bata deta hai.
Kahan / Kab
Har sale / consumption ke baad check hota rehta hai.

4Production ka Hissa

Recipe / Samagri List

Kya hai
Har banne wale product ke liye "ek piece banane mein kya-kya lagta hai" ki list — raw material aur kitni quantity.
Kyun chahiye
Production planning, material calculation aur lagat — sab isi list pe khade hain.
Kahan / Kab
Product setup ke time ek baar. Har production kaam isi ko use karta hai.

Example

Ek Steel Chair = 5 rod + 1 cushion + 8 screw. 10 chair ka kaam → 50 rod, 10 cushion, 80 screw chahiye.

Production ka Kaam

Kya hai
Ek production job — "10 Steel Chair banao". System check karta hai material poora hai ya nahi, banne par raw material stock se kat jaata hai aur finished chair stock mein aa jaati hai. Lagat aur munafa dono dikhta hai.
Kyun chahiye
Production track karne ki central cheez — kya ban raha hai, kaun laga hai, kitni lagat, faayde ka sauda hai ya nahi.
Kahan / Kab
Jab production planner decide karta hai ki ek batch banana hai.

Material Rok lena + Kaam Chalu

Kya hai
Kaam banate hi uska material "reserve" ho jaata hai — physical stock nahi hilta, bas "available" kam dikhta hai taaki doosra kaam usi material pe plan na ho. Kaam "shuru" karte hi material shop floor pe issue ho jaata hai (stock se nikal jaata hai). Ab kaam chalu hai — material gaya, product abhi nahi bana.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle material sirf kaam poora hone par katta tha. Do kaam dono possible dikhte the, phir ek material kha leta, doosra atak jaata. Ab reservation ye jhagda pehle hi khatam karta hai.
Kahan / Kab
Reserve — kaam banate hi. Issue — "Start" button dabate hi. Kaam cancel karo to material wapas stock mein aa jaata hai.

Example

2 chair ke kaam plan kiye, dono ko 50 rod chahiye, stock 60. Reservation ke baad doosra kaam saaf dikhata hai "sirf 10 rod available" — pehle hi pata chal jaata hai, production floor pe surprise nahi.

Production ke Steps

Kya hai
Ek kaam ke andar steps — Cutting → Welding → Quality Check → Packing. Har step pe kaun laga hai, kab shuru/khatam. Kaam tabhi "poora" hota hai jab saare steps ho jaayein.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle ek hi status tha. Multi-step production mein pata nahi chalta job kahan atki hai, kis step pe time laga.
Kahan / Kab
Steps optional hain — simple product ke liye chhod do, ek hi step jaisa chalega.

Reject / Wastage (Scrap)

Kya hai
Kaam poora hote time daalte ho: kitne theek bane, kitne kharab (scrap), aur kyun. Sirf theek wale finished stock mein jaate hain.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle 10 ka kaam hamesha exactly 10 deta tha. Asli production mein rejection hoti hai. Poore kaam ki lagat theek units pe baant jaati hai — scrap se per-piece lagat badh jaati hai, jo sahi hai.
Kahan / Kab
Kaam complete karte time.

Example

10 planned, material lagat ₹3000. Agar 2 scrap ho gaye → ₹3000 ÷ 8 theek units = ₹375 per piece (bina scrap ke ₹300 hota). Sahi cost tabhi dikhega jab scrap count ho.

5Vendor ko Paisa

Vendor ke Bill aur Payments

Kya hai
Jaise customer ko invoice bhejte ho, waise vendor ka bill yahan enter hota hai. Order se apne aap bhar jaata hai ya alag se. Payment record karo — kitna diya, kitna baaki. Overdue bill alag rang mein. Vendor pe payment terms (kitne din ka udhaar) aur bank details.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle purchase side ka paisa track hi nahi hota tha — vendor ko kitna dena hai, kya diya, kya late ho gaya. Ab poora hisaab.
Kahan / Kab
Jab supplier ka invoice aata hai. Order page se seedha bill bana sakte ho.

6GST — Apne Aap Sahi

GST Split (CGST / SGST / IGST)

Kya hai
Har bill/invoice pe GST apne aap sahi tarike se banta hai. Same state mein bech rahe ho → CGST + SGST (aadha-aadha). Doosre state mein → IGST (poora). "Place of supply" apne aap set ho jaata hai.
Kyun chahiye
Pehle ek flat GST line thi — GST rules ke hisaab se sahi invoice nahi tha. Ab compliant hai, invoice PDF pe proper breakdown.
Kahan / Kab
Automatic har bill/invoice save pe.

Example

Karnataka ki company Karnataka mein bech rahi → CGST ₹90 + SGST ₹90. Wahi company Tamil Nadu mein bech rahi → IGST ₹180.

7Aage Kya Add Hona Baaki Hai

Ye cheezein abhi system mein nahi hain. Har ek ke saath: matlab kya, asli example, aur kaunsi company ko chahiye.

1Product ke andar Product (Multi-level Recipe)

Abhi recipe ek level ki hai: product → raw materials. Agar koi raw material khud banaya jaata hai (uski apni recipe hai), to system usko nahi samajhta.

Example — Steel Almirah

Almirah = Door Assembly + Frame Assembly + Lock. Aur Door Assembly khud = Steel Sheet + Hinge + Handle. Abhi tumhe Door aur Frame ke alag kaam manually banane padte hain, phir Almirah ka. System nahi kehta "pehle 2 sub-part banao".

Kaun: Chhoti company — shaayad nahi. Mid company — zaroori. Furniture, electrical panel, machinery, pump, auto-parts — inme multi-level recipe aam baat hai.

2Sab Kaam ka Combined Material Plan

Reservation ke baad "available" to dikhta hai, par ek jagah "saare pending kaam mila ke kitna material chahiye, kitna kam hai" — ye plan nahi banta.

Example — 5 pending kaam

Sab mila ke 267 kg rod chahiye, stock 100 kg → ek screen batata "167 kg kam hai" aur ek click pe ek consolidated maang bana deta. Abhi ye har kaam alag dekh ke khud jodna padta hai.

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi (1–2 kaam, dimag mein ho jaata hai). Mid — bahut kaam ki cheez. 10–50 kaam khule hon to purchase team ka roz ka kaam hai. Bina iske ya to zyada kharid lete hain (paisa block) ya production rukti hai.

3Order pe Approval Limit

Maang pe manzoori hai, order pe nahi. Jiske paas order banane ki permission hai wo bade se bada order bhej sakta hai bina kisi review ke.

Example

₹5,000 tak — executive khud. ₹5,000–50,000 — manager ki manzoori. ₹50,000+ — director. Abhi ye limit system mein nahi hai.

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi (owner sab dekhta hai). Mid — haan. Purchase team badi ho to audit aur budget control ke liye chahiye. Chhota feature hai.

4Vendor ki Performance Report

Receiving ka data ab store hota hai (kitna aaya, kitna reject, time pe aaya ya nahi), par vendor-wise score-card nahi.

Example

Vendor A — time pe 92%, reject 2%. Vendor B — time pe 71%, reject 8%. Isse pata chalta B se bacho chahe rate sasta ho. Abhi ye data hai par ek jagah dikhta nahi.

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi (3–4 vendors, sab yaad). Mid — bahut useful. Data pehle se hai, sirf ek report banani hai — achha faayda kam mehnat mein.

5Debit Note (Vendor ko wapas claim)

Receiving pe reject qty note hoti hai, par vendor ko formal document nahi jaata.

Example

100 rod @ ₹100 mangaaye, 15 kharab. Vendor ne poore 100 ka bill (₹10,000) bheja. Tumhe ₹1,500 ka Debit Note banana chahiye — "itna kam denge" ya "agle bill mein adjust".

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi (phone pe adjust). Mid — haan. GST ke hisaab se ek official document hai, accounts reconciliation ke liye.

6Ek Maang → Kai Vendor ke Order

Ek approved maang se ek hi order banta hai. Maang ke items alag-alag vendor se nahi mangwa sakte.

Example

Maang mein: Rod, Paint, Bolt. Rod Vendor A se sasta, Paint B se, Bolt C se. Abhi teen alag order manually banane padenge aur maang se link toot jaata hai.

Kaun: Chhoti — workaround chal jaata hai. Mid — haan. Har material ka alag preferred vendor hota hai.

7Har Piece ka Serial Number

Abhi lot/batch level tracking hai. Har individual piece ka apna number, warranty, service history — nahi.

Example — Generator company

Customer: "Generator SN-2026-0453 kharab hai." System se pata chale: kaunse kaam mein bana, kaunse parts (unke serial), kaunsa customer, kab bika, warranty valid hai ya nahi. Batch se ye nahi milta — batch mein 50 the.

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi. Mid — sirf high-value / warranty wale saamaan (generator, machine, electronics, pump, engine). FMCG / hardware / consumable wali company ko bhi nahi.

8Kai Godown / Location ka Stock

Abhi har item ka ek total number. Kaunsa maal kaunse godown / rack mein hai — nahi.

Example

Rod total 500 kg = Pune factory 300 + Mumbai warehouse 200. Mumbai se dispatch karte time system "500 available" dikhata hai, actual wahan 200. Godown ke beech transfer track nahi hota.

Kaun: Chhoti — nahi (factory hi store hai). Mid — sirf agar sach mein kai branches / warehouses ho. Ye bada change hai — sirf demand aane par banao.

Nichod: Jo abhi bana hai, wo ek chhoti se mid manufacturing company ke liye poora kaam karta hai — material maangne se le kar finished maal bech kar paisa milne tak. "Aage kya baaki hai" wale zyadatar items sirf tab chahiye jab company mid-size ho jaaye ya khaas industry ki ho.
